Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Brooklyn Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Brooklyn and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Brooklyn

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
